# HR Analytics Dashboard 📊 | Power BI Project

Welcome to my first **Power BI** project — an **HR Analytics Dashboard** that visualizes key workforce metrics and reveals employee attrition patterns. This dashboard helps HR professionals make data-informed decisions to enhance employee satisfaction and reduce turnover.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To assist organizations in **improving employee performance** and **reducing attrition** by analyzing HR data through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

1. **Attrition by Education**  
   High attrition in *Medical and Life Sciences* indicates a need for targeted retention strategies.

2. **Attrition by Age**  
   Employees aged **26–35** and **36–45** have the highest attrition, highlighting the importance of mid-career support.

3. **Attrition by Salary Slab**  
   Most attrition occurs in the **“up to 5,000”** range, stressing the need for competitive compensation.

4. **Attrition by Job Role**  
   **Laboratory Technicians** and **Sales Executives** show the highest attrition, requiring focused engagement efforts.

5. **Attrition by Years at Work**  
   Highest attrition is among employees in their **first year**, suggesting the need for stronger onboarding.

6. **Attrition by Gender**  
   - **Male:** 60%  
   - **Female:** 40%

7. **Attrition by Marital Status**  
   - **Married:** Highest attrition  
   - **Single:** Moderate  
   - **Divorced:** Lowest
